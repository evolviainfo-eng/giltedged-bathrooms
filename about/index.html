A local, family‑run business you can trust

We’re a local, family-run business based in Billericay with a genuine passion for quality, craftsmanship and customer satisfaction. We take pride in every project, treating your home with the same care and attention as if it were our own.

At Gilt Edged Bathrooms, we take care of the whole project from start to finish. From the initial ideas and bathroom design, through to supply and professional installation, we manage all the works required to create a bathroom that looks great and is built properly.

We pride ourselves on providing a personal, reliable service and taking responsibility for every part of the job from the first conversation right through to the finished bathroom.
