How your bathroom gets done

  1. Design consultation

    A free visit to measure up and talk through what you want from the room, with design suggestions from Wayne and a free, no-obligation quote.

  2. Supply

    We supply every bathroom product, from the suite and tiles to the taps and towel rail, through our long-standing local suppliers.

  3. Installation

    Our team strips out the old room and handles the plumbing, electrics, plastering, tiling, flooring and decorating, with one person managing the project from start to finish.

  4. Pay on completion

    No deposit up front. You pay when the bathroom is finished and you are happy with everything.
